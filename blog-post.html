5 Signs You Need a Windshield Replacement

October 24, 2024 • Auto Glass

Your windshield is more than just a piece of glass; it's a critical safety component of your vehicle. While small chips can often be repaired, there are times when a full replacement is the only safe option. Here are 5 signs it's time to replace your windshield.

1. The Crack is Longer than a Dollar Bill

As a general rule of thumb, if a crack is longer than 6 inches (about the length of a dollar bill), the structural integrity of the glass is compromised. Repair resin can't effectively bond cracks of this size.

2. The Damage is in Your Line of Sight

Even a perfect repair can leave a slight distortion in the glass. If the damage is directly in the driver's line of sight, it can be a distraction or impair visibility, making replacement the safer choice.

3. There are Multiple Chips or Cracks

If your windshield has suffered multiple impacts, its overall strength is reduced. A weakened windshield provides less protection in the event of a rollover accident.

4. The Damage Extends to the Edge

Cracks that reach the edge of the windshield are particularly dangerous because they can spread rapidly and weaken the bond between the glass and the vehicle frame.

5. The Glass is Pitted

Over time, windshields can become pitted from tiny rocks and road debris. This pitting scatters light, creating glare that can be blinding during sunrise or sunset.
