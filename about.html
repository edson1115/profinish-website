About ProFinish Elite Glass & Keys

Dedicated to restoring perfection for commercial fleets.

Our Approach

ProFinish Elite Glass & Keys was founded with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, reliable auto cosmetic repairs and maintenance exclusively for fleet management companies and B2B partners.

We operate as a premium coordination network. Rather than employing in-house technicians, we find the absolute best, highly-vetted partner shops to fix these vehicles within our exclusive network.

We believe in honest pricing, transparent communication, and treating every fleet vehicle as if it were our own, minimizing downtime and maximizing efficiency for your business.
