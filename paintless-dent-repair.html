Paintless Dent Repair (PDR)

Restore your vehicle's factory finish without the need for expensive repainting.

The Best Solution for Dents and Dings

Paintless Dent Repair (PDR) is the most effective and eco-friendly method for removing door dings, creases, and hail damage. By massaging the metal back to its original shape from behind the panel, we preserve your vehicle's original factory paint.

Unlike traditional body shop methods that require body filler and paint matching, PDR is faster, more affordable, and retains the value of your vehicle.

  • Preserves original factory paint
  • Cost-effective compared to traditional bodywork
  • Quick turnaround times (often same-day)
  • Environmentally friendly process
