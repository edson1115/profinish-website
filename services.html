Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with insurance companies? + −

Yes, we work with some major insurance providers. We can help guide you through the claims process for windshield replacement and hail damage repairs to make it as smooth as possible.

How long does a windshield replacement take? + −

Most windshield replacements are completed within 60 to 90 minutes. However, the vehicle should sit for another hour to allow the adhesive to cure properly before driving.

Will Paintless Dent Removal (PDR) damage my paint? + −

No, PDR is designed specifically to preserve your original factory paint. It is the preferred method for repairing hail damage and door dings because it doesn't involve sanding, fillers, or repainting.

Do you offer a warranty on your services? + −

Absolutely. We offer a lifetime warranty on our workmanship for as long as you own the vehicle. This covers air/water leaks for glass and the permanence of dent repairs.
