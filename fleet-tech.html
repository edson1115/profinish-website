Fleet Tech Capabilities

From a single pilot unit to a nationwide fleet rollout — we handle the hardware, wiring, and configuration so your operations team doesn't have to.

GPS & Telematics

Geotab GO devices, Samsara trackers, and multi-vendor telematics hardware — hardwired or OBD-II, installed and activated.

  • ■ Geotab GO9 / GO9+ / GOtalk
  • ■ Samsara VG34 & CM32
  • ■ Verizon Connect / Mix Fleet

AI Dash Cameras

Netradyne Driveri and forward/cabin camera systems — professional mounting, cable management, and platform pairing.

  • ■ Netradyne Driveri (D-210 / D-410)
  • ■ SmartWitness / Lytx DriveCam
  • ■ Samsara dual-facing cam

Backup & Surround Cameras

Rear-view, side-view, and full 360° camera systems for vans, trucks, and trailers — hardwired with in-cab monitor integration.

  • ■ Wireless & hardwired options
  • ■ Trailer camera systems
  • ■ DOT compliance solutions

Always-On Displays

In-cab tablet mounts, ELD displays, and driver-facing screens — permanently installed with power management wiring.

  • ■ RAM / AMPS mount systems
  • ■ Hardwired USB / power ports
  • ■ ELD tablet integration

Wiring & Power Management

Clean, professional wire routing behind panels — no exposed cables, no fire hazards, no voided warranties.

  • ■ Ignition-switched circuits
  • ■ Fused direct-to-battery runs
  • ■ Weatherproof exterior wiring
